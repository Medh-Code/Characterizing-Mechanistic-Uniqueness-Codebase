**Characterizing Mechanistic Uniqueness and Identifiability Through Circuit Analysis**

This is the accompanying code to the paper "Characterizing Mechanistic Uniqueness and Identifiability Through Circuit Analysis."

Our circuit enumeration procedure was adapted from the paper, "Everything, Everywhere, All at Once: Is Mechanistic Interpretability Identifiable?" (Meloux et. al, 2025). The paper is available [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.20914) and the corresponding codebase can be found on [Github](https://github.com/MelouxM/MI-identifiability).
